KOTA

Two sides of the same file.

KOTA is where a bank's due diligence and an operator's paperwork meet. Pick the seat you want to sit in. You can change it at any point, from the mark at the foot of the sidebar.

The seat opens over the case study, at the width the screens were drawn for. Escape closes it.

Everything here is a prototype built for a portfolio. The operators, the banks, the scores and the documents are invented; the geography is real because the story is about a real place.
