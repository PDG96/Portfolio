About

(06)

Photo Replace with portrait

Hey! I'm Pietra, I design solutions that work across borders.

I started in graphic design — branding, editorial, industrial — before moving into digital product design. For the past four years I've been focused on SaaS, working with international teams that need to turn complex, regulated, multi-stakeholder domains into interfaces people can actually use.

My work tends to live at the boundary between research, information architecture and the UI itself. I map ecosystems, draw service blueprints, design role-based sidebars, and ship the screens that come out the other end — often working alongside engineering as a product architect rather than handing files over a wall.

I work in EN and PT-BR. Available for embedded engagements with startups that take their domain seriously.
